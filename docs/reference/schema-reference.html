GenerationUnitEnergyAssetInstance
Deployed generation asset with topology, dispatch, and optional dynamics data. Technology-specific design attributes live on subclasses (Hydro-, Wind-, Solar-, ThermalGenerationUnit); shared dispatch/topology fields stay here.
Carrier rule: the injected carrier is implied by atNode → CarrierDomain when a single-carrier bus is attached. Optional hasInputCarrier / hasOutputCarrier are overrides for imports or multi-fuel cases; hasOutputCarrier (or hasTechnology) is the usual carrier handle in a zonal model that uses belongsToGeographicalRegion instead of a bus. Bus classification (PQ/PV/slack) lives on ElectricalBus.powerflow_bus_type, not on the generator.

StorageUnitEnergyAssetInstance
Deployed storage that absorbs and re-releases a single carrier at one location: atNode on a NetworkNode (nodal), or belongsToGeographicalRegion on a GeographicalRegion (zonal).
A single-basin hydro plant (one reservoir, one turbine limit, natural inflow) is one StorageUnit: energy_storage_capacity, maximum_discharging_power, and annual_natural_inflow_energy with hasNaturalInflowProfile. maximum_charging_power is 0 when it cannot pump. Inflow is energy (MWh/year), the same quantity as on HydroReservoir.
A cascade, two basins, or a machine that a power flow or a dynamic study must see stays HydroGenerationUnit plus HydroReservoir. HydroReservoir is not a subclass of StorageUnit.
